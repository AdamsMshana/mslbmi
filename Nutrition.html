Nutrition Fundamentals

Discover the building blocks of a healthy diet and learn how to make informed choices about what you eat. Good nutrition is essential for maintaining a healthy weight, preventing chronic diseases, and promoting overall well-being.

Macronutrients

The Body's Fuel Sources

  • Carbohydrates: 45-65% of daily calories (whole grains, fruits, vegetables)
  • Proteins: 10-35% of daily calories (lean meats, fish, legumes, dairy)
  • Fats: 20-35% of daily calories (avocados, nuts, olive oil, fish)
  • Focus on complex carbs over simple sugars
  • Choose unsaturated fats over saturated and trans fats

Micronutrients

Vitamins & Minerals

  • Vitamin A: Vision and immune health (carrots, sweet potatoes)
  • Vitamin C: Immunity and collagen (citrus fruits, peppers)
  • Calcium: Bone health (dairy, leafy greens)
  • Iron: Oxygen transport (red meat, spinach, legumes)
  • Vitamin D: Bone health and immunity (sunlight, fatty fish)

Hydration

Essential for Life

  • Drink at least 8 glasses (2 liters) of water daily
  • Increase intake during exercise or hot weather
  • Water-rich foods: cucumbers, watermelon, oranges
  • Signs of dehydration: thirst, dark urine, fatigue
  • Limit sugary drinks and excessive caffeine

Healthy Eating Tips

Practical Guidelines

  • Eat a rainbow of fruits and vegetables daily
  • Choose whole grains over refined carbohydrates
  • Practice mindful eating and portion control
  • Limit processed foods and added sugars
  • Cook at home more often to control ingredients

Sample Meal Plan

Balanced Daily Eating

  • Breakfast: Oatmeal with berries and nuts
  • Snack: Greek yogurt with apple slices
  • Lunch: Grilled chicken salad with olive oil dressing
  • Snack: Hummus with carrot sticks
  • Dinner: Baked salmon with quinoa and steamed broccoli

Superfoods

Nutrient Powerhouses

  • Berries: Antioxidants and fiber
  • Fatty Fish: Omega-3 fatty acids
  • Leafy Greens: Vitamins A, C, K, and minerals
  • Nuts and Seeds: Healthy fats and protein
  • Turmeric: Anti-inflammatory properties

Modern Food Pyramid

🚶‍♂️

Daily Exercise

At least 30 minutes of moderate activity daily to maintain health and weight

🍎

Fruits & Vegetables

5-9 servings daily - focus on variety and color for maximum nutrients

🌾

Whole Grains

6-8 servings daily - choose whole wheat, oats, brown rice, quinoa

🥛

Protein & Dairy

2-3 servings each - lean meats, fish, eggs, legumes, milk, yogurt, cheese

🧈

Fats & Sweets

Use sparingly - choose healthy fats like olive oil, limit added sugars
