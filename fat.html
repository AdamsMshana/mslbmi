How to Measure Correctly

Follow these guidelines for the most accurate results. Use a soft, flexible tape measure.

Height

Stand upright without shoes against a wall. Measure from the floor to the top of your head.

Neck

Measure around the neck, just below the larynx (Adam's apple). Keep the tape level and relaxed.

Waist

Measure around the abdomen at the navel level. Keep the tape level and snug, but do not compress the skin.

Hip (Women)

Measure around the widest part of the hips and buttocks. Keep the tape level and parallel to the floor.
